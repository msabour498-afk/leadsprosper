Where leads get lost

Every lead moves through the same five steps. Most wasted budget comes from one weak step, not the whole account. We find that step first.

  1. Ad shownTo the right people, in the right area, for the right searches.
  2. ClickAn ad worth clicking, matched to what the person wanted.
  3. VisitA page that loads fast and answers their question.
  4. LeadA call or form that's easy to complete and gets tracked.
  5. CustomerFast follow-up, and results sent back to the ad platform.
