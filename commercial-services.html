How we bring in leads

Google Ads

Search campaigns on 'commercial cleaning', 'office cleaning', 'commercial HVAC', and similar city terms, kept separate from residential searches.

Facebook Ads

Reaching property and facility managers, and retargeting visitors with a request-a-bid offer.

ChatGPT Ads

Managers ask ChatGPT for vendor shortlists. We can test whether ads there bring bid requests.
