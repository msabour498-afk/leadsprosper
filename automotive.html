How we bring in leads

Google Ads

Search campaigns on repair and service terms by city, and vehicle ads for dealers with an inventory feed.

Facebook Ads

Inventory and service offers shown to drivers nearby, and retargeting for people who browsed vehicles.

ChatGPT Ads

Shoppers ask ChatGPT for car and repair advice. A tracked test shows whether ads there bring visits.

Common questions

What are vehicle ads?

Google ads that show a car's photo, price, and mileage from your inventory feed. They're for dealers and need a Google Merchant Center account.

Can you track walk-ins?

Partly. Calls, forms, and direction requests are tracked, and offer codes help connect walk-ins to ads.
