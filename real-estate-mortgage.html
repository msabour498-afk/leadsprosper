How we bring in leads

Google Ads

Search campaigns on 'homes for sale', 'sell my house', and refinance terms by city. Google limits targeting for housing and credit ads, so keywords and locations do the work.

Facebook Ads

Listing, home-value, and pre-approval offers, run under Meta's housing or financial products Special Ad Categories, which restrict targeting by age, gender, and ZIP code.

ChatGPT Ads

People ask ChatGPT about buying, selling, and rates. A tracked test shows whether ads there bring leads.

Common questions

Why are targeting options limited?

US fair housing and lending laws apply to ads. Google and Meta restrict targeting by age, gender, ZIP code, and similar factors for housing and credit ads.

How do you handle low-quality Facebook leads?

Higher-intent form questions, a confirmation step, and fast follow-up. We compare cost per qualified lead, not just cost per lead.
