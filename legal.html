How we bring in leads

Google Ads

Search campaigns by practice area and city, with call tracking and ads timed to your intake hours. Legal searches are some of the most competitive, so we put spend on the cases you want.

Facebook Ads

Awareness and retargeting for practice areas where people research first, like estate planning and family law.

ChatGPT Ads

People ask ChatGPT legal questions before they hire. A tracked test shows whether ads there bring consultations.

What the audit checks

  • Call tracking and intake records matched to ads

A good fit if

Common questions

Do ads have to follow bar rules?

Yes. Lawyer advertising is governed by each state's rules of professional conduct. We write factual ads, make no promises about outcomes, and you approve all copy.

Can you target people who were in an accident?

Not by their circumstances. We target the searches they make, like 'car accident lawyer chicago', in the areas you serve.
